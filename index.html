Dissertation

Does a fraud model need to know who you are?

MSc Fintech research project, National College of Ireland, September 2026. Supervised by Dr. Muhammad Umair.

Demographic Signatures in Digital Payment Fraud: A Machine Learning Framework for India's UPI Ecosystem

Public synthetic UPI dataset (Kaggle) | 250,000 transactions | 2.00% fraud rate | Python

When a model flags a UPI payment, is it reacting to who the customer is, to the bank, device and network behind the payment, or to what the transaction itself does? I split the predictors into three families (demographic, institutional and behavioural), stated three hypotheses before modelling, and tested each family on its own in a leakage-safe pipeline.

Behaviour carries the signal

Behavioural features alone scored 0.866 ROC-AUC against 0.872 for the full model, and the difference was not significant (DeLong test, p = 0.811). Demographic and institutional features on their own performed at chance.

How I built it

  1. Remove leakageDropped the free-text notes field, which restated the label, and transaction status, which isn't known when a payment is scored.
  2. Build a leakage-safe pipelineOne-hot encoding and SMOTENC resampling fitted inside the training folds only.
  3. Train and compare four modelsXGBoost as the primary model, benchmarked against logistic regression, an MLP and a TensorFlow deep network.
  4. Lock the threshold firstChose the operating threshold (0.36) on a validation set and locked it before scoring the untouched test set.
  5. Explain, calibrate and auditSHAP for explanations, isotonic calibration (Brier score 0.0212 to 0.0144) and a fairness audit across age groups.
